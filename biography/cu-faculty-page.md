## Biography

**Franklin Diaz** is a Lecturer in the Information Systems discipline at the University of Colorado Denver Business School and a seasoned technologist with over two decades of experience in DevSecOps, enterprise cloud architecture, and cybersecurity engineering.

In his professional practice, Franklin serves as a Principal Engineer and Principal Professional Services Consultant at Palo Alto Networks, specializing in advanced security architecture, enterprise threat modeling, and deployment baselines for emerging cloud and runtime environments—including Prisma Cloud and AI runtime security platforms (Prisma AIRS). His technical research centers on autonomous agent architectures, Model Context Protocol (MCP) integrations, secure sandboxing, and decentralized protocol defense. Franklin has also served as a Principal Research Engineer and Test Architect within advanced networking environments, including Indiana University's SDN Lab.

In the classroom, Franklin teaches **ISMG 2050: Business Problem Solving Tools**, translating modern enterprise engineering practices into foundational analytical skills for business students. His pedagogical approach emphasizes applied problem solving, computational reasoning, and hands-on analytical methods designed to prepare future business and technology leaders for complex, data-intensive industry landscapes.

Dedicated to technical community mentorship and adversarial defense, Franklin regularly serves as a Capture The Flag (CTF) organizer, speaker, and volunteer at leading cybersecurity conferences, including BruCON and BSidesLV.
